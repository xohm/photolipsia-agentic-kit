Radiance fields · Gaussian splats

A cloud of soft, coloured blobs.

One splat

A position, a size and rotation, a colour, an opacity. A soft ellipse in 3D.

A few

They overlap and blend, sorted from back to front for every view.

Millions

Trained from photos: a radiance field you can look around in.

A radiance field is a 3D scene learned from photos: from any point of view, it gives the colours you would see. Gaussian splats store one as soft blobs.

Files: .ply .spz .splat .ksplat .sog .rad. Spark reads all of them.

Spark keeps the splats in a tree: each parent is a softer merge of its children. It draws at most a fixed number of them (Spark 2.4.0’s built-in limits), fine close up and coarse far away: the outlined splats above the cut. So a file can hold far more splats than are drawn. Prompt 8 turns this on.
